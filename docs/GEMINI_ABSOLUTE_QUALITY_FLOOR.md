# Gemini absolute quality floor

Quality is evaluated before buildability ranking. A response may not pass
because it is valid JSON, schema-compliant, consistent, or easy to repair.

Allowed outcomes are `pass`, `pass_with_safe_normalization`,
`fail_incomplete`, `fail_conflicting`, `fail_invented_critical_meaning`,
`fail_structurally_empty`, `fail_identity_integrity`,
`fail_source_contract`, and `provider_failure`.

Packet 1 must retain portrait, landscape, and charging access and must ask
for missing phone-fit facts rather than promote invented dimensions to user
facts. Packet 2 must contain one meaningful printable holder, two trays with
276 × 184 × 44 mm dimensions and vertical top loading, the handle, drainage,
two strap slots, and mostly open walls. Packet 3 must contain meaningful slots
`1`–`4`, definite result symbols, the required dimensions and equivalent
hollow construction, and one connected unobstructed adapter.

The floor rejects empty nested objects, empty ready Plans, invalid references,
unknown geometry slots, undefined source constructs, and vacuous structured
responses.
